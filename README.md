### <img src="https://emojis.slackmojis.com/emojis/images/1563484044/6086/pepe-shrug.gif" width="25"/> What I Deliver

> **"Turning fragmented, messy data into secure, automated, and business-ready assets."**

Most data pipelines break or expose critical vulnerabilities under scale. I bridge the gap between heavy data processing and enterprise-grade security:

* ⚡ **End-to-End Automated ETL Pipelines:** Automated workflows that clean, validate, and transform raw data into optimized models.
* 🔒 **Security-First Architecture:** Your data isn’t just processed; it’s fortified. From access control to log analysis and resilient network designs.
* 📈 **Business-Driven Analytics:** Lightweight dashboards and analytical views that translate complex raw numbers into clear, decision-making KPIs.
